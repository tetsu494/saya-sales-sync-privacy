SAYA Sales Sync Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

1. Purpose

SAYA Sales Sync is a private application used by the application owner to retrieve, manage, and synchronize authorized eBay seller information for sales administration, inventory management, reporting, and accounting support.

2. Information We Access

After the eBay account owner grants authorization, SAYA Sales Sync may access eBay information necessary for business administration, including order information, item information, SKU data, sales amounts, fees, refunds, shipping information, listing information, and inventory information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through eBay is used only for legitimate business purposes, including sales management, inventory management, financial reporting, accounting support, and synchronization with private spreadsheets and internal systems.

4. Sharing of Information

Information obtained through eBay is not sold, rented, or used for third-party advertising. Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate the application and its authorized business functions.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect authentication credentials, access tokens, and business information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for authorized business, accounting, operational, or legal purposes.

7. Account Deletion and Data Removal

When eBay provides a marketplace account deletion or closure notification, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized as required by eBay policies and applicable law.

8. Revoking Access

The eBay account owner may revoke SAYA Sales Sync access through the applicable eBay account authorization settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application, legal requirements, or eBay platform requirements.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the owner of SAYA Sales Sync.
